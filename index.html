1. How to use this guide

Collection master files bring together account identifiers, loan balances, repayment information, delinquency indicators, contact history, and allocation ownership. Agents should read related fields together rather than relying on a single column.

Core rule: Verify the correct loan and customer record, check the latest available payment and allocation data, review previous contact outcomes, then follow the approved agency SOP.

Field meanings below are based on the definitions supplied for this training. Where source-system logic can vary, confirm the interpretation with the operations/data owner before using it for decisions.

3. Loan and repayment fields

Outstanding vs overdue

POS / Opening POS

POS is the current outstanding amount for the loan as represented in the source system. Opening POS is the outstanding amount at the beginning of the month.

Do not assume either amount is the overdue amount.

Amount to regularise

Collectable / Total collectable

Collectable is the overdue amount to be collected to regularise this loan. Total collectable aggregates overdue amounts across the customer's active loans.

Confirm the applicable account and approved payment instructions before communicating an amount.

Monthly commitment

EMI amount / EMI date

The EMI amount is the scheduled monthly instalment; EMI date is the customer's selected repayment date, typically between the 2nd and 7th according to the supplied definition.

Repayment progress

Paid this month / Number of EMI paid

Paid this month records the amount paid during the current month. Number of EMI paid records the instalments paid against the loan tenure.

Check the latest payment date and system update before treating a customer as unpaid.

Important data check: A payment may have been made recently but not yet reflected in a file snapshot. If the customer disputes the overdue amount or reports a recent payment, follow the payment-verification/escalation process rather than insisting the file must be correct.

7. Recommended agent workflow

  1. Confirm the record.
    Check loan_id, bank_tl, product and the approved customer verification process.
  2. Check file freshness and ownership.
    Review masterfile_date, handover_date, agency and assigned agent/telecaller details.
  3. Understand the loan position.
    Review EMI amount/date, POS, collectable, total collectable, last payment date and paid-this-month value.
  4. Review delinquency and prior contact.
    Check current_bucket, bom_bucket, repayment movement and latest call/field dispositions.
  5. Choose the next permitted action.
    Use approved prioritisation rules, contact cadence, customer communication standards and escalation paths.
  6. Record the outcome accurately.
    Update the correct disposition and notes in the authorised system. Never expose internal IDs or unnecessary personal data to the customer.

8. Practical examples

Example A — Outstanding is not the same as overdue

A file shows POS ₹30,000 and collectable ₹4,000. Explain internally that ₹30,000 is the outstanding loan balance as represented by the system, while ₹4,000 is the overdue amount identified for regularisation. Confirm the live system before quoting a payable amount.

Example B — Payment reported after file generation

The masterfile_date is two days old and last_payment_date appears older than the payment the customer says they made yesterday. Check the authorised payment ledger or raise a verification request before continuing collection activity.

Example C — BOM bucket and current bucket differ

bom_bucket reflects the bucket at allocation/month start; current_bucket reflects the current bucket according to the supplied definition. A difference can indicate movement during the month. Confirm system refresh and bucket logic before interpreting it.

Example D — Multiple active loans

total_collectable and total_pos are customer-level aggregates across active loans. collectable and POS are specific to the loan record. Always identify the relevant loan before discussing an account-level amount.

Example E — Previous contact outcome

If calling_latest_disp_code shows a recent contact outcome, review the notes and follow the permitted contact cadence. Do not repeat contact unnecessarily or misrepresent an earlier promise or outcome.

9. Data privacy and professional conduct

Protect customer information

Use customer data only for authorised collection activity. Do not share phone numbers, DOB, email, internal IDs, or loan details with unauthorised persons.

Communicate respectfully

Be factual and professional. Do not threaten, shame, harass, disclose debt to unrelated third parties, or make unsupported claims about consequences.

Verify before acting

When information conflicts, use approved systems and escalation routes. A master file is a snapshot, not necessarily a real-time ledger.

Keep records accurate

Never fabricate call attempts, visits, promises to pay, payments, or dispositions. Follow client, agency, and applicable regulatory requirements.
